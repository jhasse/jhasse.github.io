Addictive JezzBall Arcade Fun

Sweet Split takes the classic JezzBall arcade challenge and gives it a fresh twist: draw your lines in ANY direction – not just up, down, left or right! Drag your finger at any angle to split the bouncing sweets apart and fill 75% of the free space to clear the level! Sounds easy? Every level adds one more sweet to dodge. The board gets crowded, the bouncing gets wild, and one wrong line costs you a life. How far can you make it?
